Visit & Explore

Start at the Quechee Gorge Visitor Center

Managed by the Hartford Area Chamber of Commerce in partnership with the Army Corps of Engineers and the Vermont Department of Forests, Parks, and Recreation, the Visitor Center is a welcoming first stop for visitors discovering the region.

Located within Quechee Gorge State Park, the center welcomes more than 100,000 visitors each year and helps connect travelers to local businesses, attractions, and services throughout the Hartford Area and Upper Valley.

Current Visitor Center hours: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily.

Who we are

A Stronger Chamber for a Stronger Community

The Chamber is committed to attracting, supporting, and growing businesses throughout the greater Hartford Area and Upper Valley. From startups and small local businesses to established employers, it helps members thrive through advocacy, economic development initiatives, community visibility, and practical business resources. Located on the Vermont–New Hampshire border, it proudly serves Quechee, White River Junction, Hartford Village, West Hartford, Wilder, and surrounding communities.
