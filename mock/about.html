About Us

A stronger Chamber for a stronger community.

Our Mission

The Hartford Area Chamber of Commerce is committed to fostering a thriving economic environment where local businesses and the community prosper, through value-added services and programs that help businesses succeed in Hartford, Vermont.

Our Vision

We proudly serve the five villages of Hartford: Quechee, White River Junction, Hartford Village, West Hartford, and Wilder. Membership extends to Hanover, Norwich, Woodstock, Hartland, West Lebanon, and Windsor County.
