Why don't you sell day passes?

The grounds are open rain or shine, but launches depend on safe weather. A weekend pass lets you come on multiple days, which gives you more chances to see balloons fly, and keeps prices accessible. Youth in foster care and participating schools receive passes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Soar over the Quechee, Vermont landscape!

When will I see balloons?

Hot air balloons are on the field only during scheduled flight times, and during the Balloon Glow celebration on Friday and Saturday. Weather permitting, there will also be a tethered balloon offering rides.

How long is a hot air balloon ride?

Adventure Rides last around 2 hours, with the flight itself lasting 40–50 minutes. Upon landing, you'll have the chance to take part in the tradition of sharing a bottle of champagne with fellow passengers. You may also be asked to help roll up the envelope (the balloon) and store it in the basket before the chase vehicle transports you back to the field.

Are there age restrictions for flying?

The pilot has the final decision on accepting or declining a passenger to ensure everyone's safety.
Senior citizens: must be in good physical health, able to climb into and stand in the basket. Landings can occasionally be rough, so take caution.
Children: cannot be held and must be able to stand in the basket and see over the edge, typically requiring a height of at least 45 inches. Children must be accompanied by an adult.

How do you decide if the balloons will fly?

Our Balloonmeister has the final say. We make every attempt to get balloons into the sky, and flights can be delayed by up to 2 hours based on wind and rain.

What should I wear for a hot air balloon ride?

Wear comfortable shoes, like sneakers, and dress in layers.

How many people can fit in a balloon?

Balloons can typically carry up to 8 people per ride, depending on weight and basket size. Groups of more than 4 may be split up, but we do our best to accommodate larger parties. We recommend booking in advance for larger groups.

How much does a balloon ride cost?

A balloon ride is $425 per person, plus tax, and includes full admission to the festival.

Are balloon rides available for purchase during the festival?

Rides typically sell out in advance of the festival. If any remain, we will offer them for sale on the festival grounds starting when the gates open on Friday.

How do I purchase a tethered ride?

Tethered rides are available for purchase on-site during the festival for $30. They are offered throughout the festival (weather permitting, most often during dawn and dusk). These rides go up to 100 feet in the air while remaining secured to the ground with ropes. They are first-come, first-served, with in-person tickets only.

Do you offer balloon ride refunds?

Before June 1st: full refund minus a $50.00 admin fee.
After June 1st: we are unable to offer refunds.
If cancelled by us: claim your refund in person at the Balloon Booth, and be prepared to show your ticket.
All refunds take 7–14 days to process.
