Why don't you sell day passes?

The grounds are open rain or shine, but launches depend on safe weather. A weekend pass lets you come on multiple days, which gives you more chances to see balloons fly, and keeps prices accessible. Ticket prices haven't changed in years.

Free and discounted passes

Passes are offered to local youth in foster care and participating schools. Children under 12 are eligible for discounted admission.
