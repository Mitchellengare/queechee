3 · Covered Bridges of Windsor County

Fifteen covered bridges, a short drive apart

One of Vermont's most beloved sights. Here are four near Quechee.

  • Quechee Covered Bridge · Waterman Hill Rd. Steel with a wood covering; rebuilt after Hurricane Irene in 2013. Crosses the river waterfall, a favorite spot for watching balloons.
  • Taftsville Covered Bridge · Woodstock. Built 1836, 186 feet, one of Vermont's longest.
  • Lincoln Covered Bridge · Woodstock. Built 1877, 136 feet, a registered US historic site.
  • Middle Covered Bridge · Woodstock. Built 1877, rebuilt 1969 in lattice-and-peg style; survived a fire in 1974.

4 · Area History and Information

Hartford's five villages

Hartford Village

A historic hub on the White River

Once made chairs, tools and textiles. Today a walkable village with swimming holes and the Upper Valley's only dog park.

Quechee

From mill town to resort

Settled in the 1760s around river mills. The Gorge draws over 200,000 visitors a year.

White River Junction

From railroad hub to arts district

Galleries, professional theater, music venues and restaurants downtown.

West Hartford

Rural charm on the river

Kayaking, tubing and fishing, a country store, library and the new Patriot's Bridge.

Wilder

A planned community

Named for Charles Wilder. Home to the Wilder Dam and Salmon Ladder.
